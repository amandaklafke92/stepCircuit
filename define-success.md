# Define success

Captured: 17 September 2026.

This records the outcomes discussed with Amanda. It is not a technical specification. Suggested evidence below is a starting point for joint scoping; numerical targets and acceptance thresholds have not been agreed.

## Working goal hierarchy

**Working primary aim:** complete a meaningful IoT learning experiment and create credible portfolio evidence through a functioning wearable prototype. Amanda indicated that learning and portfolio value may appropriately be the main goal; the exact balance remains open to refinement.

**Personal usefulness:** securely worn step tracking with access to the data without a required subscription. The first version can be tested alongside the existing tracker.

**Stretch aim:** become good enough to replace Amanda's Oura ring for everyday step tracking, with automatic updates to her Google Sheet and/or web app so steps no longer require manual entry. Replacement refers to step tracking, not sleep, recovery, or Oura's other features.

## Three dimensions of success

| Dimension | Desired outcome | Possible evidence to agree during scoping |
|---|---|---|
| Personal usefulness | A wearable that stays attached, feels comfortable, and provides accessible step data without a required subscription | Wear trials; comparison against manually counted walks; reading data on the iPhone; an honest record of charging and sync behaviour |
| Learning | Understand and participate in a first IoT build, including hardware, device software, phone connectivity, and AI-assisted development | Amanda can explain the path from movement sensor to recorded steps; assemble and test the device with guidance; describe debugging discoveries and trade-offs |
| Portfolio | Demonstrate building beyond web apps and show judgement, ownership, and verification | A working demonstration, photos, selected decisions and tests, limitations, and clear attribution of Amanda's and AI's work |

## Blender experiment

Amanda wants to explore a 3D, IKEA-style assembly walkthrough: actual parts, an exploded view, and ordered instructions showing how the hardware goes together.

Its intended roles are an assembly aid, a learning experiment in AI-assisted 3D work, and a possible portfolio artefact. A useful future check would be whether Amanda can follow the instructions to assemble the selected hardware and identify where instructions need correction. A polished render alone would not demonstrate that the electronics work or that the assembly is correct.

## Stretch success: everyday replacement and automatic data entry

- The device is sufficiently accurate, comfortable, secure, and convenient to charge for Amanda to choose it for everyday step tracking.
- Step data reaches her chosen Google Sheet and/or web app automatically, without manually typing the daily total.
- The access workflow does not require a step-data subscription. This does not imply zero hardware, tooling, hosting, or possible app-distribution costs.

We have not yet defined what "automatically" means: background syncing, syncing when an app opens, or another trigger. The destination, frequency, offline handling, duplicate prevention, and iPhone feasibility need later investigation. No integration has been selected or authorised for implementation by this discovery capture.

## Questions to resolve before technical commitments

- What would make the first wearable prototype worthwhile, even before everyday replacement?
- Which learning and portfolio outcomes matter most to Amanda?
- How large or noticeable can the piece be, and what styles would she enjoy wearing?
- What budget, available tools, and time commitment fit the experiment?
- What counting error, battery life, and charging routine would she accept for a prototype versus an everyday replacement?
- What real-life activities should be included in testing?

Amanda is leaning towards an anklet (17 September); the final choice remains open pending research and testing. A thin ring is not the current focus. No screen, battery size, sensor, circuit board, app framework, cloud service, or waterproofing requirement has been chosen.
