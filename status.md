# StepCircuit status

Discovery: motivations and the working success framework are captured; initial anklet research is recorded in `anklet-options.md`. Read `AGENTS.md` for orientation. No hardware or app implementation has been started.

The repository is prepared for public visibility. `PUBLICATION.md` defines the public/private boundary, and raw recordings and unreviewed personal media under `content/` stay local by default.

## What's established

- Amanda uses an iPhone and wants to learn through an IoT wearable build.
- The project serves personal usefulness, learning, and portfolio evidence; see `define-success.md`.
- Replacing Oura for step tracking and automatically updating a sheet/web app are stretch ambitions.
- Collaboration should support Amanda's decision-making and meta-learning; see `project-framework.md`.
- The proposed kit product is named **StepCircuit**: “Build your own step tracker.” See `stepcircuit-product-concept.md`.
- Its MVP will not include an AI support agent. Amanda plans 3D IKEA-style assembly instructions; programming guidance is still to be chosen, with a QR-linked demo video the leading first option.
